About DyeMera

Our Mission

Illuminating Discovery, Together

DyeMera develops fluorescent labeling technology that helps researchers and clinicians see what was previously invisible. Since our founding patent on LipoFLUDDY, we've grown from an academic collaboration into a team building validated tools for imaging, drug screening, and clinical diagnostics.

We work closely with university hospitals, research institutes, and accelerator programs across Germany to bring our fluorophore technology from the lab bench into real research and clinical workflows.
